# Changelog

## 1.3.0

### Added

- Restored AppleSkin, AmbientSounds, Entity Culling, and Sound Physics Remastered now that they support Minecraft 26.3.

### Changed

- Ported to Minecraft 26.3.
- Updated Complementary Unbound to r5.9.3.
- The CurseForge edition omits Sounds because its 26.3 dependency chain is not available there.

### Removed

- Removed Sit because Minecraft 26.3 now provides its behavior.
- Removed Bobby, Cut Through, Cubes Without Borders, Particle Effects, and Zoomify because they do not have Minecraft 26.3 releases.

## 1.2.0

- Bundled Complementary Unbound as the pack's only shader.
- The CurseForge edition does not include Presence Footsteps because no Minecraft 26.2 file is available there.
